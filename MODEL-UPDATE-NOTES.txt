BRIARFORD — TRIPO ENCOUNTER MODEL UPDATE
October 31, 2026

INSTALL
This is the complete game bundle, based on Briarford-Unlocked (2).zip.
Upload this ZIP as the Netlify site bundle, or extract it and serve the folder.
index.html is at the archive root. Keep the assets directory with the game.
The live hosted game has not been changed by this local update.

MODELS INSTALLED
Champion's Challenge: revised silver-and-blue Champion body, separate blue
shield and separate sword attached to its animated hands. The sword's extra
lower guard is narrowed locally during preparation. Original rejected Champion
is not included in the game.
Hollow Champion: the approved dark armored model, a separate sword, and an
outer armor layer that disappears at the existing 55% health transition.
Ravenous Mass, Buried Colossus, Carrion Queen, Hungry Earth: approved Tripo
models replace their procedural encounter bodies.
Wild Hunt: all five pack wolves use the approved gray wolf model.
Death Bell and Drowned Host retain their existing art. Other wolf summons and
campaign bosses are not changed by this encounter-specific update.

ANIMATION AND PRESENTATION
Local lightweight skeletal controls animate walking, wolf gait, Champion
attacks, wings, jaws, head movement and Colossus hand strikes. Death poses are
handled separately. These are procedural animations, not paid Tripo clips.
Meshes are aligned to the game's forward axis and scaled for their encounter.
Hungry Earth is shortened to fit its ground-mouth role.
Colossus bone weights use its texture to keep soil still during hand movement.
Imported textures are capped at 1024 pixels on mobile, using the existing
texture-recovery path. Per-instance geometry/materials/skeletons are cleaned
up when encounters leave or corpses expire.

SPAWN CORRECTION
Encounter objectives can initialize their source enemy inside the active
event's reserved slots without being blocked by normal enemy-type limits
or the early-game elite lockout. The overall population cap still applies.
Normal wave spawning retains its existing limits.
