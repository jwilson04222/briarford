Boss and encounter VFX alignment

Reference direction: Dragon Shot, organic poison/oil pools and the Thorn
Matriarch's rooted, shaded geometry. The referenced hero effects and the
Matriarch's root artwork are preserved.

Tempest Oracle: replaced disconnected thin zigzags and rapid opacity flicker
with connected branching strokes, dark edges, blue body and bright core.
Branches connect to the main discharge and stay within the warned lane width.
Short ground traces and batched sparks finish the hit. Crossing lanes and
Forked Verdict both use the replacement; the old lightning branch is removed.

Storm Channeler: small strikes and the final strike use the same new electrical
art. Damage and warning timing remain unchanged; cosmetic decay lasts slightly
longer so the contact is readable.

Ground impacts: Grave Quake, enemy slams/leap landings/boulders and the Buried
Colossus fist impact now use an irregular dark ground mark, shaded stone slabs
and low dust. No perfect flat disc or floating rock ring.

Drowned Host: replaced three thin lines with a curved, shaded liquid crest,
foam ridge and droplets. The warned area and damage remain unchanged.

Validation: four sampled animation phases for each effect at mobile viewport
size; actual Oracle and Matriarch move cycles; warning-before-hit assertions;
actual Storm Channeler small and final strike route; repeated geometry/texture
cleanup; direct-file HTML gameplay startup and pause/resume.

This patch changes the effects listed above. It does not certify every effect
in the game as final. Browser QA does not replace native-device testing.
