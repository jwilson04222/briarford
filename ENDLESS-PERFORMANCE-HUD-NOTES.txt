ENDLESS / COMBAT HUD UPDATE

XP is beneath health inside the player card, with a percentage or an explicit
XP ready / next choice countdown. The top-of-screen XP stripe is removed.
Endless continues banking XP during its existing 25-second upgrade-choice gap.
No XP, upgrade, health, enemy-count or difficulty balance values were changed.
Event panels use more horizontal space, reducing wrapping, and clear the
player card. Boss/event coexistence retains separate panels.

Ordinary lich beams now use the spectral attack renderer. Dread Fiend,
flame turrets and other shared flame jets use inked flat-color flames rendered
in a single instanced draw per jet. Removed the rejected flame-sheet asset.
Attack timing, range and damage are preserved.

PERFORMANCE
Static opaque pieces of affix ornaments are merged by material at creation.
Animated pieces, icons, translucent fields and material changes remain intact.
In a controlled 76-enemy scene with 16 affixes, additional affix draw calls
fell from 180 to 108 (40% less); total calls in the matched comparison fell
from 372 to 300. Triangle counts were unchanged. All eight affixes passed
material and geometry comparisons at three animation states.

Encounter model preprocessing now occurs during loading. Colossus preparation
previously performed a 1024x1024 canvas pixel read during its first spawn.
The software-rendered test showed a severe synchronization stall there; after
moving preparation to loading its first measured spawn took 9.7 ms. This is
not a claim of an equivalent time saving on a physical phone or iPad.

TEST COVERAGE AND LIMITS
44 phone/tablet HUD layouts; 5 command layouts; earned-XP, waiting and level-up
sequence; actual lich and Dread Fiend attack routes; 21 spawn/cleanup cycles
(7 new model encounters repeated 3 times). Geometry and texture counts stayed
at 54 and 52 after warmed encounter cleanup, with no increase across repeats.
Crowd stress scenes up to 76 actual enemies. No browser exceptions in checks.
GPU-heavy crowds and transparent effects remain possible causes of slowdown.
No native iPad/Android FPS measurements or hour-long live runs were performed.
