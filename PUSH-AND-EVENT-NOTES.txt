BRIARFORD SURVIVORS — KNOCKBACK AND EVENT PRESENTATION

BONE SHAPER
- Bone Halo and Bone projectiles from death effects no longer push enemies.
- Halo damage, Bleed, cage hits and returning-rib interactions are retained.
- The previous Life Drain healing cap remains in place.

TRAPPER
- Shotgun knockback applies once to each enemy per blast, instead of once per
  pellet. Regular pellet pushes also have a 0.6-second recovery per enemy.
- Single-blast force is 18 at early ranks and 32 from rank four (previously
  45 or 120 PER PELLET). All pellets still deal their normal damage.
- Explosive pellet splash deals damage and burn without pushing nearby mobs.
- Shrapnel receives only a light impact. Breach shots retain a stronger,
  separate 55-force shove with a 0.75-second recovery.
- Trap holds, trap blast force and spring-corpse impacts are retained.
- Clockwork's gun and other builds' projectile rules are unchanged.

EVENT POWERS
- Last Footfall: an opaque, textured spectral double follows the player's
  flank, turns toward targets, and switches between running and attacks.
  It fires bright spectral blades with an impact effect. It retains the
  existing attack count, damage and cooldown; movement can change coverage.
- Carrion Bloom: hinged jaws, visible dark mouth, teeth, leaves and a stem.
  It snaps and recoils on each damage pulse, with bite audio. Its rank-three
  finishing bite retains the acid burst.
- Cinderheart: a charred heart with molten fissures and flame tongues. A
  visible heartbeat accelerates before detonation. Each pulse produces
  Dragon Shot-style fire; the finishing blast has its own impact sound.
- Six event-power slots and later mastery upgrades remain available.

VALIDATION
- Before/after projectile and halo hit tests: damage matched while the
  targeted knockback decreased. Boss/juggernaut immunity and Clockwork
  regression checks passed. Repeated-push recovery and volley limits checked.
- All 13 current builds were checked for clone movement and attack animation.
- Three event powers checked through their cast, pulse and finish sequences.
- These are focused regression and presentation checks, not full Endless
  balance certification or physical-device performance measurements.
