COMBAT HUD READABILITY UPDATE — October 31, 2026
Based on Briarford-Unlocked-New-Models.zip with the Trapper targeting fix.

PRESENTATION
- Combat panels now use the menu's comic heading font, ink borders, deep blue
  backgrounds and gold accents. Body instructions use clear high-contrast type.
- Health and level share one compact panel. Health numbers are larger; low
  health changes their color and the panel border without a flashing effect.
- Timer, gold and kills have a compact panel with readable numbers.
- Boss names and attack warnings are larger. HP values remain exact. Twin boss
  information remains supported.
- Endless cards separate the encounter title, countdown badge, progress and
  instructions. Instructions are retained in full. A countdown under ten
  seconds changes badge color. Death Bell retains toll/soul progress rather
  than replacing it with objective health.
- Boss and event cards share a row when both are active. Temporary notices
  are positioned below active cards instead of overlapping them.
- Temporary alerts regain clear headings and a remaining-time underline.
- Command labels and cooldown badges are larger and higher contrast. Longer
  Fury/Sunder status uses two lines above the button. Button hit areas and
  movement controls are unchanged.

VALIDATION
- Syntax validation passed.
- 44 layout cases at 667x375, 844x390, 1024x768 and 1280x800: normal HUD,
  all nine event cards and simultaneous boss/event/notice display.
- Checked panel bounds, text overflow, boss/event separation and notice layout.
- Five command layouts checked at the smallest viewport: Greatsword, Sword
  and Shield, Sonic Bard, Clockwork and Demolition. Included high level/health
  values, long command status, cooldown text and badge overlap assertions.
- Screenshots inspected in the actual WebGL game. No browser runtime errors.
- Viewport tests do not measure native iPhone/iPad/Android performance.

PRESERVED
All model and audio assets, encounter integration, Trapper targeting changes,
combat balance, progression and save rules are unchanged.

UPLOAD
Upload the full ZIP to Netlify. index.html remains at the archive root.
New version tags on game.js and combat-hud.css avoid the old cached UI.
