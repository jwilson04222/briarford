BRIARFORD SURVIVORS — BOSS FLOW & PURSUIT

DREAD FIEND
Replaces the generic leap, three fire lines, and Cinder Wake with two connected, grounded attacks.
Twin Claws: two sweeping claw strikes, each with its own locked cone warning. The second swing aims again before its warning begins. The rear is safe; contact follows the visible sweep, with one hit per swing. The original dual-wield animation is staged through anticipation, contact, and follow-through.
Furnace Rush: a marked, grounded rush accelerates and decelerates along a fixed route. A separate warning marks the torn trail before its fissure erupts. The trail uses dark molten cracks, rock fragments, and embers. It does not create persistent fire pools or a projectile fan.
Below half health, the second swing warning and fissure warning become slightly shorter. All phases retain warnings and recovery opportunities.
Close-range claw attacks are only selected when the target is actually within reach.

BISHOP OF DUSKMOOR
Predator's Bite: stalking reduced from 1.1 to 0.42 seconds; a fixed 0.85-second lane warning precedes a smooth grounded lunge. Collision uses the swept route rather than only checking the endpoint. A hit no longer abruptly stops the lunge.
Night Flight: the curved bat route now passes through the player's position locked at the start of the warning. Flight accelerates and decelerates smoothly; the whole route is warned first.
Blood Tithe: two servants approach during a maximum 3.6-second feeding window, replacing three servants and up to nine seconds of inactivity. Killing both before either feeds, or dealing 2.5% of the boss's maximum health during the channel, interrupts the feast. Denial grants a 1.5-second stagger and vulnerability window. Successful servants heal at most 1% each, with a 4% lifetime cap on this move's healing.
Blood Hunger: below 40% health, the bite gains a second lunge with a fresh 0.8-second warning. No instant retargeting during either lunge.
These two bosses' special cooldowns now advance during their attacks and recoveries, rather than silently adding that time to every cooldown. Only one special can be active at a time.
Both reworks are shared by campaign and Endless spawn definitions. Boss health and attack damage have not received a general stat increase.

BOSS PURSUIT & CROWD CONTROL
Boss slow cap reduced from 80% to 25%. All normal and elite enemy slow behavior is retained.
Repeated roots can hold a boss for at most 0.4 seconds before a two-second resistance window. This also covers direct root applications from traps, shield attacks, and leap attacks, which previously bypassed the stun resistance path. Roots and stuns share the resistance window.
Fear briefly staggers a boss instead of making it flee. Existing boss freeze-to-brittle behavior and Shatter opportunities are retained.
Bosses using the regular movement controller approach 20% faster. Above 160 units of separation they accelerate into a pursuit run, settling back to normal approach speed within 90 units. Pursuit uses the same movement and collision controller; it does not teleport, modify the player's speed, shorten warnings, or accelerate committed attacks. Bespoke scripted event movement is not rewritten by this rule.

VALIDATION
Attack sequences checked at 30, 60, and 120 simulation steps per second: resolution, movement continuity, bounded hits, fixed aim, safe rear/side positions, separate follow-up warnings, feed interruption/healing cap, cancellation, cleanup, and terrain stopping.
Repeated-root tests at all three rates held bosses for approximately 2.0–2.2 seconds over twelve seconds of continuous root applications, rather than the entire twelve seconds.
Controlled eight-second straight-line pursuit comparisons covered Dread Fiend, Bishop, Undead Knight King, Rotting Corpse, and Ancient Lich, with and without maximum slows. These are movement tests, not simulated human clear rates.
Full game-loop attack and movement checks, mobile Chromium render checks, and a staged real-engine movement preview. Physical iPhone/iPad play remains necessary to judge the final feel.
