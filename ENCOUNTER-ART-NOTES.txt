Champion and animal encounter presentation update

Champion: replaced the generated model with the existing rigged Paladin skin,
recolored in dark steel and burgundy. Uses existing sword/shield props and clips.
Hollow Champion: replaced the generated model with the existing Black Knight
skin, recolored in cold steel/green. Its shoulder armor disappears at the
existing armor-break phase. Both skins retain their original game silhouettes.

Champion attack clips now follow the windup, hit and recovery timing.
Wolf model retained. Added articulated lower legs and paws, distance-driven
diagonal gait, anticipation and landing compression, and committed lunge facing.
Carrion Queen model retained. Added wing segments, takeoff, banked turns,
dive folding, landing flare and recovery. Removed instant flight-height changes.

Steel, claw, dive and armor-break impacts now use focused slash, dust and metal
effects instead of the previous generic impact treatment. Other encounters'
effects are outside this patch; this is not a complete game VFX replacement.

No HP, damage, reward or progression changes. Smooth turning changes animal
movement paths slightly. Existing attack warnings and hit timings are retained.
Previous XP/HUD, spectral lich and fire-rendering changes are included.
Champion recolor and outline preparation now happens during loading, rather
than when the first champion appears in combat.

Validation: browser simulations of all four encounter attack cycles, committed
attack-facing checks, bird-height continuity and Hollow armor-break checks;
visual inspection of movement samples and attack phases. This is browser QA,
not a native iPad/Android device test or a substitute for visual approval.
