EVENT REACH & TRAPPER FOCUS

Faultline: a rolling rockslide travels substantially farther, with two overlapping lanes at rank two and returning aftershocks at rank three. Overlapping lanes cannot multiply damage to the same enemy on one pass.
Winter’s Teeth: five piercing ice fangs travel outward in a spreading volley. Rank two increases the fan to seven larger, longer-reaching fangs. Rank three adds two shards on the first impact. Overlapping fangs do not shotgun a boss with the entire volley.
Executioner’s Wake: a broad blade curves outward, then returns to your current position at every rank. Rank two increases width and reach. Rank three adds a weaker second crossing blade.
Stormglass is unchanged.

Automatic event powers now have compact icon countdowns at the bottom center of the battlefield. The final second is highlighted. A ready power waiting for a target reads READY. Rock, ice, and blade also build up briefly beside your character before firing. The display hides during pause and menus.

Trapper: visible bosses and event objectives within actual pellet range get explicit priority over ordinary packs. Focus survives a brief defensive shot at imminent contact and resumes when that pressure clears. Dead, phased, offscreen, and out-of-range targets lose focus immediately. Expiring Locked N’ Loaded correctly reduces targeting range. Manual right-stick aiming still overrides auto aim. Gun damage, pierce, cooldowns, gear, and enemy stats are unchanged.

Validation: 75 reward/rank runtime cases; near and far target hits; fork/volley overlap caps; blade return to a moving player; six-icon HUD; pause/cleanup; 180-second effect stress test; Trapper old/new focus comparison, range/visibility/phase changes, manual aiming, and actual projectile delivery through one blocker. Browser checks do not substitute for physical iPhone/iPad testing.
