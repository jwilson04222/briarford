BRIARFORD SURVIVORS — ENDLESS EVENT EXPANSION

Carrion Queen
- Hunts in flight, with stronger wing strokes and a banking body pose.
- Dive warning reduced from 1.2 to 0.7 seconds. Direction locks for the dive.
- A missed dive grants a 2.1-second grounded opening with 35% increased damage taken.
- A connected dive recovers for 0.65 seconds, without that vulnerability bonus.
- Attackable during flight. No added invulnerability or HP increase.
- Repeated roots, freezes and stuns slow her flight instead of stopping the phase cycle indefinitely.

25 event powers; six equipped at once
- Added Iron Comet: a returning spiked ball; rank three adds a second ball.
- Added Ravenwheel: ravens prioritize visible elites/bosses, gain piercing dives, then regroup for a second pass.
- Added Faultline: a moving rupture, branching paths, then a return eruption.
- Added Ricochet Relic: an iron idol rebounds between enemies and can strike isolated bosses repeatedly.
- Added Razorwake: moving leaves damaging toothed traps; higher ranks widen and repeat their bite.
- Added Gallows Pendulum: a chained axe sweeps, reverses, then adds a focused chop.
- Added Siegebreaker: a targeted anvil drop, a second drop, then piercing impact fragments.
- All new powers use automatic nearby targeting, their own icons, toon shading and smooth outlines.
- New powers do not add knockback or permanent crowd-control barriers.
- Six equipped powers remains the limit. Three reward choices at a time. Mastery upgrades continue after rank three.

Reworked powers
- Carrion Bloom burrows between nearby targets and lunges to bite; rank three adds a smaller hunter.
- Last Footfall performs visible lunging sweeps; rank three attacks from both flanks. Uses the current hero's animated model.
- Cinderheart follows the hero, charges, then sweeps flame across nearby enemies. Rank three adds a return pass.

Validation scope
- All 25 powers checked at ranks 1, 2 and 3 for activation, damage, rendered objects and actor cleanup.
- Carrion Queen exercised against deliberate dodges, deliberate hits and repeated crowd control.
- Visual checks use the actual game renderer and models.
- This is a mechanics/presentation pass, not proof of final Endless balance or physical iPad/Android performance.
- Prior Bone healing, Bone/Trapper knockback fixes and testing unlocks retained.
