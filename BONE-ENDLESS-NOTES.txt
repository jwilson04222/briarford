BRIARFORD SURVIVORS — BONE SUSTAIN / ENDLESS POWER SLOTS

Bone Shaper
- Life Drain healing is capped across ALL targets per cast: 2%, 4%, 6%,
  8%, then 10% of maximum HP at ranks 1–5. It still requires actual damage.
- Damage, targeting, cooldown, cage protection and Bone Halo are unchanged.
- This addresses damage-scaling full heals, not a proven invulnerability bug.
- Plaguebringer and other Necromancer stances retain their existing Life Drain.

Endless
- Carry six automatic event powers, increased from three.
- Rewards still show three choices. Until all six slots are occupied, choices
  include new powers alongside an upgrade to an existing power.
- Once full, choose among three owned powers to upgrade. Three behavior ranks
  and repeatable mastery remain; six is the slot limit, not the rank limit.
- Briefing, reward text and pause loadout now describe the six-slot limit.

Validation scope
- Before/after Life Drain measurements and deliberate crowd-contact probes.
- Healing caps at all five ranks, empty-target and overkill cases, plus Plague
  regression check. All 18 powers tested in three six-power groups at rank 3.
- These are focused regression tests, not full-run Endless balance certification.
