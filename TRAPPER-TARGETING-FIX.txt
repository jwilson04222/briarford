TRAPPER AUTO-AIM FIX — October 31, 2026
Based on the supplied Briarford-Unlocked-New-Models.zip.

FIX
Removed the Trapper gun's indefinite target retention, including its stronger
Locked N Loaded lock. Nearby threats can now replace a distant living target.
Visible reachable enemies take priority over offscreen enemies. Nearby packs
take priority over distant targets, including caught enemies and bosses.
Within that group, the gun chooses useful shotgun-fan coverage, with modest
boss/trapped-enemy preference and aim stability between similar targets.
The selector reconsiders every 0.12 seconds; immediate contact threats bypass
that delay. Both barrels refresh their target before creating pellets.
Two-thumb manual aiming still overrides automatic selection. A reachable
stolen soul keeps its special attack priority. Lone distant enemies remain
valid fallback targets while pellets can reach them.

SCOPE
No damage, knockback, gear, cooldown, map or progression values were changed.
Clockwork's target-selection behavior remains unchanged.
All supplied encounter models, rigging and assets are preserved.

TESTS
Ten before/after target-selection cases: normal and hot-load distant locks,
caught-target and boss locks, immediate contact, dead targets, offscreen
fallback, hot-load expiration, aim stability and Clockwork regression.
Actual shotgun projectile directions checked for both barrels and manual aim.
Ten-second real game-loop checks at 844x390 and 1024x768, with real skeletons,
projectile collisions and rendering. Test health inflated to isolate targeting.
These checks verify targeting and runtime behavior, not long-run class balance
or native-device performance. Test hooks are not included in the game.

INSTALL

The game script URL has a new version tag to avoid the old cached script.
