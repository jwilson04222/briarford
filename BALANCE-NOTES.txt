HALBERD MOVEMENT AND IMPALE FIX

Hook & Hew and empowered Blood Slam allow normal movement throughout the
cast. The pull/chop/slam still resolve around the cast's original position.
Damage, cooldowns and invulnerability timing are unchanged.

Impale follows WASD/arrows or movement stick, including camera yaw.
Stationary input uses the right aim stick, current mouse cursor, or facing.
PC mouse support works even though this HTML uses the mobile game mode.

Bosses, elites and other control-resistant enemies receive the existing
impact hit without truncating the charge. They cannot be carried.
The eight-target carry cap, cooldown and normal 210.8-unit distance remain.
Walls, water, steep terrain and world boundaries still block the charge.

VFX: clamp the charged-weapon rim calculation before fractional exponentiation
to prevent negative inputs; spectral weapons no longer cast opaque shadows.
The exact reported full-screen flash was not reproduced in frame captures.
This is a rendering stability attempt, not a confirmed complete flash fix.

Regression checks reproduce the old movement lock and enemy-stop defects;
patched checks pass in desktop and touch browser contexts. Actual PC right
click and WASD + right click were checked. Normal/empowered effects were
captured frame-by-frame. Not a physical-device test or full balance sweep.

Clockwork's automatic shotgun and all temporary testing unlocks are retained.
Demo balance has not changed; user's Duskmoor V feedback is queued for a
focused IV-versus-V investigation.

BRIARFORD SURVIVORS — AFFIX PRESENTATION & BOSS COMBAT PASS
October 31, 2026

AFFIX PRESENTATION
All eight enemy affixes now share authored illustrated badges between menus and combat. Each menu card explains the effect and the counter. The pause guide opens directly, with a scrollable card list and separate controls in phone landscape. Endless starts its briefing with explanations rather than an unexplained name list. Campaign difficulty and rolled mutator explanations use the same readable layout.
Affixed enemies receive distinct body details: a rally standard, coin satchel, fangs, phase veils, linked cuffs, heavy shoulders, directional shield, or hourglass and marked Null boundary. Support tethers use outlined streams; Bonded uses physical chain links. Recipient badges no longer stack directly over the primary affix badge.
Rot Pods use a pulsing organic model. All gas trap placement paths use the canister model. Existing acid-pool presentation is preserved.

BOSS TIMING & ATTACKS
Boss warnings are generally 15% shorter. Minimum times preserve large repositioning moves, soul reaping and leaps. Both shared leap users move continuously through a parabolic arc to a locked, terrain-valid landing destination.
Undead Knight King: Execution March warning 1.2s -> 0.85s; advance 1.8s -> 1.05s; thrust tell 0.85s -> 0.32s; recovery 1.35s -> 1.15s. Direction stays locked.
Attack animation staging advances into the contact frame at the end of the warning. The thrust windup now advances instead of leaving its animation paused.
Rotting Corpse: replaces poison horseshoe with three successive bile spits. Each landing is marked and locked; later spits target your new position. Puddles last four seconds. Inhale still leaves a safe rear opening and clears that boss's previous hazards when it begins.
Dread Fiend: replaces fire horseshoe with Cinder Wake, three advancing marked fire bands.
Ancient Lich: replaces fire horseshoe with Soul Shear, two perpendicular soul lanes through the initially marked position. Soul Reaping remains.
Quake follow-ups run on game time instead of browser timers, synchronizing warning, damage and pause behavior.

VFX
Boss earth impacts now reach the upgraded effect path previously restricted to elites. Falling meteors use the upgraded meteor renderer. Boss weapon strikes use inked moving arcs. Fire turret jets originate at the muzzle and use the included transparent flame artwork with animated flow, shared with fire breath/lines. Soul lanes use flowing trails and sculpted skulls. Hostile fire pools now use scorched ground instead of spinning legacy flame decals.
Artwork: assets/comic-flame-jet.png, generated using the built-in image tool and integrated as a real runtime texture. The standalone HTML embeds it.
This is not a claim that every boss mechanic is unique: Troll King and Gallows Brute still share slam/quake families. Existing Bishop and three Endless-exclusive boss mechanics remain, with tighter telegraphs. Endless event mechanics were not redesigned in this patch.

PLAGUE
Removed undocumented Wither pulse knockback for Plaguebringer. Its overlapping slows use the strongest effect instead of adding toward immobility. Rank-four Aura of Decay applies its advertised 60% slow. Damage, aura radii, enemy health and progression formulas are unchanged.
Controlled durable-enemy reproduction: before, 0/30 enemies crossed 15 units inside the aura in 30s. After, 30/30 crossed within 5s and 26/30 reached melee range by 10s. This isolates movement behavior, not clear-rate balance.

VALIDATION
31 attack paths across the twelve-boss Endless roster exercised in the live browser engine, with rendered frames inspected. Targeted reruns cover changed quake scheduling, fire rendering and soul lanes.
Continuous airborne positions and height verified for both leap users. Knight King reached thrust impact at approximately 2.25s versus the former 3.85s.
Actual update-loop bile test: standing still lost 172 HP; moving away from marked positions lost 0 HP under the same controlled setup.
Phase transitions, bond separation, Null inside/outside behavior, support buffer bounds, poison models, pause/resume touch interaction and three mobile viewport layouts checked. Chromium viewports 844x390, 800x360 and 1024x768. Not physical iPhone/Android hardware testing or a fresh full balance matrix.
Previous Virulence display and Halberd cleave fixes retained byte-for-byte.
All previous package assets preserved; one new flame texture added. Existing test unlocks remain enabled.
